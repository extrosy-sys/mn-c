Privacy Policy — SilkShutter

App published by Extrosy LLC · Last updated: 5 November 2026

The short version: SilkShutter does not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal data. Your photos are processed entirely on your device and never leave it unless you share them yourself. The only network use is Google Play's purchase system, for the optional 4K and 8K upgrade.

What data we collect

None. SilkShutter has no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking of any kind. We have no server for it to send anything to.

Camera

SilkShutter needs camera access to capture the live frames it stacks into a long-exposure photograph. Camera frames are processed on your device, in memory, and are never uploaded anywhere. Finished photos are saved only to your device's own photo gallery (in the Pictures/SilkShutter folder).

Location (optional, off by default)

SilkShutter includes an optional "Geotag photos" setting that is turned off by default. If — and only if — you turn it on and grant location permission, the app reads your device's current location and embeds it into the saved photo's standard EXIF metadata, exactly as an ordinary camera does. This location data is written only into your own photo file, stored on your device. It is never transmitted, collected, or shared by us. You can turn the setting off at any time, or revoke location permission in Android Settings.

Photo storage

Photos you save are stored in your device's gallery. They stay on your device under your control. SilkShutter does not have any server, cloud storage, or backup service.

Third parties

SilkShutter contains no advertising networks or analytics services. Its one third-party component that uses the network is Google Play's purchase system (Play Billing), which is why the app holds the internet permission. It talks only to Google Play, to sell and restore the optional 4K and 8K upgrade; Google handles that under its own privacy policy, and we never see your payment details. We share no data with anyone, because we collect none in the first place.

Children

SilkShutter is a general-audience camera utility and is safe for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect data from children or anyone else.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "Last updated" date.

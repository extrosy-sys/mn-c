3. What the app does to your network and your data plan

  • Data use. Speed and load tests transfer a large amount of data — hundreds of megabytes in a full check on a fast line. The connection monitor and the scheduled check keep using small amounts for as long as they run. Charges from your provider or carrier are yours; use "Skip the speed test" on metered connections.
  • Router settings. The router check briefly asks your router (by UPnP or NAT-PMP) to open a test port and then removes it; the "Two devices" waiting side asks the router to open its port for the session and closes it afterwards. A router that does not behave as the standards say may keep such an opening; you can remove it on the router's own settings page.
  • Battery and background work. The monitor and the two-device test run until you stop them, and the optional scheduled check runs every few hours until you switch it off.
